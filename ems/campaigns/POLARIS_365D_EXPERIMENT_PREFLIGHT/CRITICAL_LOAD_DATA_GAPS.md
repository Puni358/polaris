# CRITICAL-LOAD DATA GAPS REGISTER

## Unresolved Station-Specific Data Gaps

1. **Essential Heating Power Ratings ($P_{\text{heating,min}}$)**:
   - **Missing Information**: Minimum electrical power $[W]$ required to prevent structural freezing and sustain habitability.
   - **Required Source**: Physical equipment audit or electrical single-line diagram from Swedish Polar Research Secretariat / SITES.
2. **Water Treatment Power Ratings ($P_{\text{water,min}}$)**:
   - **Missing Information**: Potable water filtration, purification, and pumping power ratings $[W]$ and minimum duty cycles.
   - **Required Source**: Station engineering drawings or water system equipment nameplates.
3. **Emergency Communications Power Ratings ($P_{\text{comms,min}}$)**:
   - **Missing Information**: Continuous electrical power $[W]$ for emergency radio, satellite link, and telemetry infrastructure.
   - **Required Source**: Installed telemetry hardware specifications.
4. **Electrical Distribution Circuit Mapping**:
   - **Missing Information**: Single-line electrical diagram mapping station breaker channels to essential heating, water, comms, and noncritical loads.
   - **Required Source**: Station electrical panel schematic.
5. **Empirical Station Electrical Demand Time-Series**:
   - **Missing Information**: Continuous measured station load $[kW]$ time-series.
   - **Required Source**: Sub-metered station electrical data.
