## 3. AUTHORITATIVE HUMAN DECISION PRINCIPLES (SEALED)

1. **STOP AUTOMATIC OPTIMIZATION**: Upon encountering `UNKNOWN_INVALID`, the system MUST immediately halt automatic EMS optimization dispatch.
2. **PRESERVE PROTECTED-CRITICAL SERVICE**: Protected-critical loads (essential heating required for human survivability, essential water filtration, emergency communications) must be preserved via physical hardware fallbacks.
3. **REQUIRED OPERATOR INTERVENTION**: Authorized operator intervention is required to evaluate station conditions and restore operation under separately governed conditions.
4. **NO AUTOMATIC EMERGENCY CLASSIFICATION**: `UNKNOWN_INVALID` does NOT equal `EMERGENCY`. `UNKNOWN_INVALID` signifies that ECD cannot establish a trustworthy survivability assessment; `EMERGENCY` signifies a validated physical crisis.

6. **NO AUTOMATIC RECOVERY**: Automatic recovery from `UNKNOWN_INVALID` to `NORMAL` is NOT authorized until a separate recovery policy is governed (`AUTOMATIC_RECOVERY_TO_NORMAL = NOT_AUTHORIZED`).
7. **TEMPORARY SOFTWARE CONTAINMENT**: The pre-existing runner behavior (`severity="CRITICAL_TERMINATION"`, `response="FAILED_TERMINATED"`, `state_advanced=False`, loop break) is recognized as temporary software containment. It does NOT establish physical station safety.
