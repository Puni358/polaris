# POLARIS EMS — REGIONAL RELATED EVIDENCE AUDIT

## 1. Executive Summary

This document evaluates regional research infrastructure evidence (`REGIONAL_RELATED_EVIDENCE`) concerning the **Abisko Scientific Research Station** complex.

> [!CAUTION]
> Information concerning the main Abisko facility must **NOT** be assumed to apply directly to remote field monitoring sites on Mt. Nuolja without explicit verification.

---

## 2. Regional Evidence Register

### Source SRC-004: Statens fastighetsverk (National Property Board of Sweden)
- **Document**: Abisko Facility Heating Modernization Project Report.
- **Established Facts**:
  - Abisko main station historically relied on direct electric radiators due to low electricity tariffs.
  - Facility is transitioning to geothermal heating (main building) and air-source heat pumps (auxiliary residential buildings).
  - Solar PV panels installed on main facility roofs for summer field season support.
- **Nuolja Applicability**: Regional context only. Does NOT establish electrical heating kW ratings for Nuolja protected loads.

### Source SRC-005: Umeå University / ANS Operations Summary
- **Document**: Abisko Scientific Research Station Operational Overview.
- **Established Facts**:
  - Approximately 40% of the station operating budget is dedicated to electricity (heating, lighting, lab instruments).
  - Station enforces energy conservation guidelines during winter field research.
- **Nuolja Applicability**: Regional context only. Does NOT provide measured kW load profiles for Nuolja.

### Source SRC-006: EU-POLARIN Research Infrastructure Description
- **Document**: Abisko Station Access & Infrastructure Profile.
- **Established Facts**:
  - Describes laboratory, workshop, and vehicle assets (e.g. Skoda Enyaq EV).
  - Remote field sites on Mt. Nuolja are accessed for transient research monitoring.
- **Nuolja Applicability**: Regional context only.
