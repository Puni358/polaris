## 2. Digital Twin Simulator Responsibilities

1. **Setpoint Ingestion**: Consumes `DispatchPlan` generated by EnergyOptimizer or Option D Policy Manager.
2. **Ground-Truth Execution**: Simulates physical state transition using actual renewable/demand values $A_t$.
3. **Energy Accounting**: Tracks ground-truth battery energy $E_{\text{bat}}$, fuel volume $F_{\text{rem}}$, served load $P_{\text{served}}$, unmet load $P_{\text{unmet}}$, and curtailment $P_{\text{curt}}$.
4. **State Feedback**: Returns updated `StationState` for timestep $t+1$ to advance the closed-loop MPC simulation.
