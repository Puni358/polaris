# Primal Degeneracy Analysis

In renewable surplus conditions, solar generation and wind generation are both unpriced renewable energy sources ($c_4$ penalizes curtailment equally for both).
Any combination of solar and wind serving load or battery charging results in identical $J_1=J_2=J_3=J_4=0$.
This represents primal degeneracy, where multiple primal decision vectors achieve the exact same lexicographic objective values.
