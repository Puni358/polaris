3. FORENSIC SYNTHESIS: MODEL VS OUTPUT-CONSTRUCTION LIMITATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Why does N6 output 24 points?
   The upstream feature matrix `candidate_k_2024_clean_matrix.parquet` contains
   exactly 24 rows per origin (for `horizon_hour` 1 through 24).
2. Why is index 0 labeled $t_0 + 0\text{h}$?
   The authors of Stage 8.1 corrected pipeline explicitly defined `horizon_hour=1`
   as $t_0 + 0\text{h}$ (`origin_dt + timedelta(hours=h-1)`).
3. Consequence for Horizon 24 ($t_0 + 24\text{h}$):
   Because the feature matrix provides only 24 rows ($h \in [1, 24]$), the model
   evaluates predictions for $t_0 + 0\text{h} \dots t_0 + 23\text{h}$. It does NOT
   evaluate a prediction for $t_0 + 24\text{h}$.
4. Nature of the Limitation:
   The inability to provide an N6 model forecast for $t_0 + 24\text{h}$ is an
   *output-construction and feature-availability limitation*, NOT an intrinsic
   architectural inability of gradient-boosted trees to predict a 25th lead hour.
