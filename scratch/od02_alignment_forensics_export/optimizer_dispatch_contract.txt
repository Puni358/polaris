1. Fixed Discrete Interval ($\Delta t = 1.0\text{ h}$):
   - Step $t \in \{0 \dots 23\}$ models an operational duration of 1 hour.
   - Power variables ($p_{\text{sol\_used}}, p_{\text{wind\_used}}, p_{\text{diesel}}, p_{\text{chg}}, p_{\text{dis}}$)
     are continuous rates in Watts, converted to Wh energy by multiplying by $\Delta t = 1.0\text{ h}$.
   - Thus, the optimizer treats $p_{\text{sol\_max}} = \text{solar\_gen}[t]$ as the *average power*
     available over the 1-hour interval $[t_0 + t\text{h}, t_0 + (t+1)\text{h}]$.
2. Step 0 Operational Horizon:
   - Physical interval: $[t_0, t_0 + 1\text{h}]$ (e.g. 00:00:00Z to 01:00:00Z).
   - Initial condition: $E(0) = \text{initial\_e\_wh}$ (measured at origin $t_0$).
   - Terminal state of step 0: $E_{\text{next}}[0]$, representing the battery stored energy at $t_0 + 1\text{h}$.
3. Step 23 Operational Horizon:
   - Physical interval: $[t_0 + 23\text{h}, t_0 + 24\text{h}]$ (e.g. 23:00:00Z to 24:00:00Z).
   - Terminal state of horizon: $E_{\text{next}}[23]$, representing stored energy at $t_0 + 24\text{h}$.
   - Layer C constraint enforces: $E_{\text{next}}[23] + s_{J2}[23] \ge \max(E_{\text{ebr}}[23], E_{\text{term}}) + E_{\min}$.
4. Multi-Channel Symmetry within GAP19:
   - Inside GAP19's formulation, all four resource channels (`solar_gen[t]`, `wind_gen[t]`,
     `demand[t]`, `critical_demand[t]`) and reserve floor `ebr_target_wh[t]` enter
     identically at index $t \in [0, 23]$.
   - GAP19 itself imposes *no shift* on any channel. Any shift between solar, wind,
     and demand is introduced *strictly upstream in the adapters* (`adapters.py`).
