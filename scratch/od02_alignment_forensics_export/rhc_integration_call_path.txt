3. ANALYSIS: RE-FORECASTING VS ALIGNED-ARRAY CACHING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Single-Shot Mode (N6ForecastProviderAdapter):
   - At step 0 of each origin, `self._engine.predict()` generates the 24-hour raw forecast.
   - `_align_n6_bundle_od02` is called ONCE and the aligned bundle is stored in `self._cached_bundle_by_origin[origin]`.
   - Subsequent steps $1 \dots 23$ do NOT re-run prediction; they slice the cached aligned bundle.
2. Receding-Horizon Mode (N6RecedingHorizonForecastAdapter):
   - At every step $k \in \{0 \dots 23\}$, `self._engine.predict()` is called FRESH with the current simulated station state and step-specific feature matrix `steps[step_index]`.
   - `_align_n6_bundle_od02` is called FRESH on the newly returned `raw_bundle`.
   - It takes the aligned bundle's hour 0 (`step_view(aligned_bundle, 0)`), which corresponds to raw row 1 of the fresh prediction.
   - Thus, in receding-horizon mode, the adapter is applied FRESH to each newly generated 24-hour prediction, NOT to an already rolled/sliced array from midnight.
