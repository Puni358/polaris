## 5. RAW NWP INTERPRETATION

The Raw NWP forecast (`fc_ws_10m`) exhibits severe systematic overestimation bias ($+18.79\,\text{m/s}$ average bias, $\text{MAE} = 18.8024\,\text{m/s}$).

### Domain Interpretation:
This high error DOES NOT prove that the underlying AROME/ECMWF numerical weather prediction model is intrinsically poor. Rather, it demonstrates that raw NWP grid cells represent high-altitude mountain peak model terrain ($1,000-1,500\,\text{m}$ a.s.l.) surrounding Abisko, creating a severe vertical elevation and microclimate mismatch when evaluated uncorrected against valley floor Abisko ground observations ($392.2\,\text{m}$ a.s.l.). The evaluated ML formulations substantially reduced the observed bias relative to the raw NWP configuration under this test setup.
