## Physical Coverage & Nuolja Topography

Nuolja ($1,160\text{ m}$ peak) rises directly to the west-southwest of Abisko valley ($385\text{ m}$ a.s.l.) and Lake Torneträsk ($341\text{ m}$ a.s.l.). The SITES Nuolja Middle Station is situated at $675\text{ m}$ a.s.l. on the eastern slope of Mount Nuolja.
