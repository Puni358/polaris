## A. Scope and restrictions

The underlying investigation was strictly read-only, investigating exactly two questions:

1. **Historical MEPS LWP/IWP availability** — were the MEPS liquid-water-path/ice-water-path products Candidate F depends on actually published and accessible at each forecast-origin decision cutoff?
2. **Training integrity** — were Candidate F and its ensemble-derived inputs trained and evaluated without evaluation-period contamination, target leakage, or other train/evaluation boundary violations?

No model retraining, inference, planner/solver execution, EMS replay, campaign run, or broader Candidate F audit was performed as part of that investigation or in writing this record. This document does not independently re-verify the underlying investigation's findings beyond what is restated here — it preserves what was found and cites what was cited, without re-running any check.

## B. Executive verdicts

1. **Historical MEPS LWP/IWP availability: `UNRESOLVED_NO_HISTORICAL_AVAILABILITY_EVIDENCE`.** No local evidence proves the same-day 00Z MEPS cloud-microphysics product was, or was not, available at the forecast-origin decision cutoff in 2024/2025. Absence of publication evidence is **not** treated as proof of unavailability, and the only retrieval evidence found (a 2026 re-download from today's archive) is **not** treated as proof of historical real-time availability.
2. **Training integrity: `TRAINING_CONTAMINATION_VERIFIED` for the 2025 ensemble-derived inputs specifically**, with the following scope distinctions, all explicitly preserved and not collapsed into a single blanket verdict:
   - Candidate F's own GBDT weight-fitting step: `TRAINING_INTEGRITY_VERIFIED` for that step alone.
   - 2024 ensemble parameters (`k_h`/`w_h`): `TRAINING_INTEGRITY_VERIFIED` relative to their documented 2024-EVEN/ODD split.
   - 2025 ensemble parameters (`k_h`/`w_h`): `TRAINING_CONTAMINATION_VERIFIED`.
   - The four 2025-instantiated derived feature families (`pred_stage3b_cloud_nwp`, `phys_pred_fold`, `pred_stage4_fold`, `disagreement_fold`): `TRAINING_CONTAMINATION_VERIFIED`, as a direct, inherited consequence of the 2025 parameter contamination — not an independently-discovered second defect.

**This contamination invalidates the claim that Candidate F's reported 2025 result is a clean, genuinely blind evaluation. It does not, by itself, establish that every Candidate F prediction in every period, or any future deployment of a corrected model, is contaminated** — the GBDT weights themselves, the 2024 evaluation, and the LWP/IWP availability question are each separate findings with their own status, not swept into this one.

## C. Feature-availability findings (Check 1)

Three features were investigated: `lwp_latest`, `iwp_latest`, and `lwp_x_cloud` (derived from `lwp_latest × cloud_frac`).

- **MEPS source variables:** LWP from `mass_fraction_of_cloud_condensed_water_in_air_pl`; IWP from `mass_fraction_of_cloud_ice_in_air_pl`.
- **Same-instant initialization concern:** the relevant MEPS run is tagged and fetched as the **same calendar day** as the forecast origin itself (`run_init_00z = origin_dt`, i.e. `== forecast_start`) — not a prior-day cycle. This was confirmed in both the live 2024/2025 extraction code (`stage6/stage6_6_lwp_iwp/run_stage6_6.py:214,223-230,245-246`) and an independent 2020–2023 reconstruction (`stage7/stage8/station_feedback_adaptation/candidate_L_research/acquire_extract_meps_2020_2023.py:140-148,228-233,245-247`).
- **+1 through +24 horizon semantics:** a dedicated semantics audit proved these are **per-horizon forecast trajectory values** from that one same-day run (valid times `origin_dt+1h .. origin_dt+24h`), not a static pre-origin snapshot repeated across horizons — `stage7/stage8/stage8_11_lwp_iwp_semantics/STAGE8_11A_FEATURE_SEMANTICS_AUDIT.md:60-84`.
- **Alternative vintage (18Z) check:** the 18Z query used by the same acquisition code requests only `air_temperature_2m`, `relative_humidity_2m`, `cloud_area_fraction`, `x_wind_pl`, `y_wind_pl` — it never requests the LWP/IWP variables at all, in either the live or the 2020–2023 reconstruction script. This means the question "could a prior-day cycle have supplied these fields causally?" was never tested by this codebase, one way or the other — it remains open, not answered negatively.
- **The critical distinction preserved from the underlying investigation:** *missing historical publication evidence is not proof of unavailability.* No archived catalog metadata, request log, or contemporaneous ingestion record with a genuine historical publication timestamp was found anywhere in the repository. The only retrieval-related evidence is a 2026-dated re-download of the archive, which the investigation explicitly declined to treat as proof of 2024/2025 real-time availability. The verdict is therefore `UNRESOLVED_NO_HISTORICAL_AVAILABILITY_EVIDENCE` for all three features, not a downgrade to "unavailable."
- `lwp_x_cloud` inherits this unresolved status from `lwp_latest`; its own construction (`stage7/stage8/stage8_1_corrected_2025/build_corrected_feature_matrix.py:127`) introduces no independent leak or clean-availability evidence of its own.

## D. Training-integrity findings (Check 2)

- **Candidate F's final model (GBDT weights):** trained on `X_tr = even_day[CANDIDATE_F_38_FEATURES]` only (`stage7/stage8/stage8_1_station_tuning_research/phase2_candidateF_additive_feature.py:143`), evaluated on `odd_day`/`day25`, with no evidence found of the model being fit on either evaluation set. **`TRAINING_INTEGRITY_VERIFIED` for this specific fitting step.**
- **2024 ensemble parameters** (`stage4/stage4_results/ensemble_parameters.json`, supplying `k_h`): fit via `optimize.minimize` on `tr_h_day["actual_SW_IN"]` where `train_mask = df["forecast_start"].isin(even_origins)` restricted to 2024 origins (`stage4/run_stage4_pipeline.py:156-160,271-285`); the script's own banner states "All learned parameters fitted ONLY on train data" (`stage4/run_stage4_pipeline.py:14`). **`TRAINING_INTEGRITY_VERIFIED`, relative to the documented 2024-EVEN/ODD split** — this file is used only for 2024 rows (`build_corrected_feature_matrix.py:61-62`) and says nothing about 2025.
- **2025 ensemble parameters** (`stage4/stage4_results_2025/ensemble_parameters.json`, supplying `k_h`/`w_h`): the EVEN/ODD split here is built from **2025** origins (`stage4/run_stage4_pipeline_2025.py:200-203`), and `optimize.minimize` fits `w_opt`/`k_opt` directly against `tr_h_day["actual_SW_IN"]` — **realized 2025 solar irradiance** — at `stage4/run_stage4_pipeline_2025.py:304,317-341`, with the result written straight to the 2025 parameter file at `stage4/run_stage4_pipeline_2025.py:347-348`. `CANDIDATE_F_REPORT.md:8-15` (same folder as this document) states that 2025 "is used whole, as the blind set" — meaning the 2025-EVEN origins whose realized targets were used to fit these parameters are inside the set the report calls genuinely blind. **`TRAINING_CONTAMINATION_VERIFIED`**: for the 2025-EVEN subset of the blind set, this is same-fold target leakage (the row's own realized target informed the parameters later used to construct that same row's input features); for the 2025-ODD subset, it is a milder but still real within-year-fitting concern (not same-row leakage, but the same calendar year's realized outcomes informed parameters later applied within that year).
- **Four derived feature families for 2025 rows** (`pred_stage3b_cloud_nwp`, `phys_pred_fold`, `pred_stage4_fold`, `disagreement_fold`): the feature-matrix builder explicitly branches to the 2025 parameter file (`stage7/stage8/stage8_1_corrected_2025/build_corrected_feature_matrix.py:73-74`) and computes these four features directly from its `k_h` (`build_corrected_feature_matrix.py:148-156`). **`TRAINING_CONTAMINATION_VERIFIED` for the 2025 instantiation of these four features**, as a direct consequence of the 2025 ensemble-parameter finding above. The same four feature families, computed for 2024 rows from the clean 2024-only parameter file, are not implicated by this finding.
- **Scope boundary, explicitly preserved:** this contamination affects the *inputs* fed to Candidate F for 2025 rows and thus the validity of the reported "blind 2025" evaluation metric. It says nothing about the GBDT model's own weights (fit only on 2024-EVEN, found clean) and nothing about the 2024-only evaluation (found clean on its own terms).

### I.3 External documentation on operational publication latency

- **MET Norway/MetCoOp wiki pages** (`github-wiki-see.page/m/metno/nwpdocs/wiki/meps-dataset`, `.../NWPdocs/wiki/Data-access`): confirm product naming and archive folder structure (hierarchical by year/month/day, matching the project's URL pattern), but **state no explicit publication-latency figure** and name no THREDDS catalog field that records a publication/creation time distinct from the nominal run label.
- **External search** located a MetCoOp conference poster ("The Operational Forecast Process at MetCoOp") stating that **MEPS forecast output reaches users approximately 135 minutes (2h15m) after the nominal analysis time.** This is a provider-partner-published operational figure, not a THREDDS-archive-specific or 2024/2025-year-specific measurement, and it describes general output dissemination rather than THREDDS ingestion specifically — both caveats are preserved here rather than treated as settled fact.
  - Source: [The Operational Forecast Process at MetCoOp](https://bibliotheque.meteo.fr/pub/DOC00061566-the-operational-forecast-process-metcoop.html)
- Separately, a documented 2020 incident (MET Norway) shows the 00Z and 06Z MEPS ensemble runs of 2020-01-13 were delayed and unpublished for hours, with users advised to fall back to an earlier forecast — direct historical evidence that MEPS publication timing is **not instantaneous and not always on-schedule**, reinforcing that a same-instant-as-init assumption is unsafe in general, independent of the specific 135-minute figure.
  - Source: [status.met.no incident history](https://status.met.no) (referenced via web search; specific incident page not individually re-fetched in this session)
- **Archive regime change:** MET Norway's own 2023 notice states the MEPS archiving approach was changed ("the old archive approach did not scale") and enforced from **2023-09-30** — meaning the archive's own internal structure/retention changed partway through the 2024/2025 period this investigation concerns, a fact noted here as a limitation on generalizing any single day's catalog behavior across the whole 2024–2025 window.

### I.4 Verdict applied to the actual decision cutoff

- **Decision cutoff used:** `forecast_start` = 00:00 UTC (the project's own matrix structure has the `horizon_hour=1` row's `timestamp == forecast_start`, confirmed in the original audit; **this is an inference from data structure, not an explicitly named "decision_time" variable in the code** — flagged as such, not treated as independently verified beyond that inference).
- **MEPS 00Z run initialization:** confirmed (code + live catalog) to equal `forecast_start` exactly — the run and the decision cutoff are the same instant.
- **Logical lower bound, independent of any specific latency figure:** a numerical weather prediction run cannot publish its own output at the exact instant it initializes — some strictly positive processing-plus-distribution time is physically required. This holds regardless of the magnitude of that time.
- **Magnitude, from the strongest external evidence found:** ~135 minutes (MetCoOp, §I.3) — meaning realistic availability around 02:15 UTC, roughly 2h15m **after** the 00:00 UTC cutoff.

**Verdict — `lwp_latest`: `UNAVAILABLE_AT_CUTOFF_VERIFIED`.**
**Verdict — `iwp_latest`: `UNAVAILABLE_AT_CUTOFF_VERIFIED`** (identical source, identical reasoning).
**Verdict — `lwp_x_cloud`: inherits `UNAVAILABLE_AT_CUTOFF_VERIFIED`** from `lwp_latest`.

**This revises the original Section C/E verdict (`UNRESOLVED_NO_HISTORICAL_AVAILABILITY_EVIDENCE`) upward in confidence to `UNAVAILABLE_AT_CUTOFF_VERIFIED`**, on the strength of: (a) the physical/logical impossibility of zero-latency publication at the exact same instant as run initialization, combined with (b) an externally-sourced, provider-partner-published operational latency figure (~135 min) that is not THREDDS-archive-specific or year-specific to 2024/2025, but is the strongest documented figure located. **Residual uncertainty, explicitly preserved:** no artifact-level, 2024/2025-dated, `meps_det_pl`-specific publication timestamp was found (the one genuine 2024-dated timestamp located, for a different lagged-ensemble product, was explicitly not generalized here); the decision cutoff itself is inferred from data structure, not read from an explicit code constant; and the 135-minute figure is a general operational figure, not a per-year or per-archive-regime measurement. If a future investigation locates a `meps_det_pl`-specific, 2024/2025-dated publication record showing materially different timing, that would supersede this revision.

### J.D Minimum safe successor (specification only — nothing below is implemented or authorized)

- **Features removed/replaced:** `lwp_latest`, `iwp_latest` (and derivatively `lwp_x_cloud`) would be re-sourced from the prior-day 18Z `meps_det_pl` run instead of the same-day 00Z run. No other feature in the 37+1 contract is affected.
- **Naming:** `lwp_latest`/`iwp_latest` would become **misleading** under a vintage change — per Section I's own semantics audit, "latest" already refers to a per-horizon forecast trajectory, not an observation, but changing the source run changes *which* trajectory. A renamed feature (e.g. `lwp_18z_fc`, `iwp_18z_fc`) is recommended so the feature contract itself documents its own provenance, rather than silently changing the meaning of an existing name.
- **Feature contract / model inputs / artifacts / training code — must all change.** This is not a drop-in substitution: changing the source data for two of 38 inputs changes the training distribution those inputs were fit against. **The current frozen `candidateF_elevation_x_cloud.joblib` cannot legitimately accept these replacement features** — it was trained on 00Z-sourced values, and substituting 18Z-sourced values at inference time without retraining would silently feed the model a distribution it never saw, independent of either source's own causal status. This is stated as a direct requirement, not assumed.
- **New training artifact required:** any such replacement requires (a) a new feature-matrix build (new file, new hash) using the 18Z source, (b) a newly trained model (new `.joblib`, new SHA-256, not a modification of the existing one), (c) the same reproducibility checks already used for Candidate F (hash verification before training, exact feature-count/order checks) applied to the new artifact.
- **Truly untouched blind evaluation set:** the new model's 2025 "blind" evaluation must not reuse `stage4/stage4_results_2025/ensemble_parameters.json` as currently constructed — per the existing, separately confirmed finding (Section D above), that file's `k_h`/`w_h` were fit on realized 2025 `actual_SW_IN`. **This contamination must be corrected first** (re-fit those parameters using only pre-2025 data, or reuse the 2024-fit parameters forward) — otherwise any new 18Z-based model's "blind" 2025 result would inherit the same contamination the original Candidate F result has, independent of whether the LWP/IWP causality problem is fixed.
- **Gates/governance required before any Arm B integration:** per the EXP-THREE-ARM-2024 experiment's own rules (`experiments/exp_three_arm_2024/scripts/gates_three_arm.py:37`), any new arm must be explicitly added to `ELIGIBLE`/`E1_holm_family` by the project lead before its results could count toward promotion eligibility; a separate, explicit execution authorization (its own solve ceiling, its own single-use reference) would be required before any run; and the full S1–S4/E1 gate set would apply unchanged, exactly as specified in the prior Causal Arm B Feasibility work. None of this is performed, requested, or implied by this report.

**The replacement source being causally plausible does not, by itself, make the model valid** — both the model-retraining requirement and the 2025 ensemble-parameter contamination must be resolved independently before any blind evaluation of an 18Z-based successor could be trusted.
