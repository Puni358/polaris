## 3. Absolute campaign blockers (must ALL be resolved before ANY N6 multi-origin campaign may execute)

1. **Row 1** — a valid, signed, campaign-specific authorization. (Still fully absent. The existing proposal is explicitly unsigned and non-binding.)
2. **Row 2** — authoritative initial-state evidence for every origin the campaign would actually use. (Currently zero origins qualify.)

No other row in Section 2 is an absolute precondition for *executing* a correctly-scoped, honestly-labeled offline comparison — but every other row is an absolute blocker for treating that comparison's output as a *production-promotion* basis (see the right-hand column).

## 4. Campaign eligibility verdict

**The campaign is not eligible to execute today, under any scope.** Row 1 alone is sufficient to keep it locked (`replay_harness.py`'s guard is unconditional and this pass added no bypass), and Row 2 independently keeps it locked even if Row 1 were somehow satisfied (no origin has valid initial-state evidence). This verdict holds regardless of how many of the production-promotion-only rows are eventually resolved — those rows govern what a completed campaign's results could be *used for*, not whether the campaign may run.

**This matrix does not unlock the campaign.** It is a diagnostic/planning artifact. Unlocking requires, at minimum: (a) a real, signed authorization record satisfying Row 1's schema, independently reviewed and approved by the project owner acting as "project lead" (and a separate governance-authority sign-off if results will ever be used for a promotion claim), and (b) a real, cited, validated initial-state table for every origin named in that authorization's `authorized_origin_set`.
