## 2. CORE AUDIT PRINCIPLE & CLASSIFICATION SYSTEM

Every substantive claim in the research package was evaluated against six mutually exclusive audit categories:

1. **`SOURCE_FACT` (Category A)**: The cited source explicitly supports the claim in the stated context.
2. **`CONTEXT_LIMITED_FACT` (Category B)**: The source supports the claim only for a particular device, protocol, or environment.
3. **`ENGINEERING_INFERENCE` (Category C)**: The source does not explicitly state the POLARIS claim, but the inference is reasonable and derivable.
4. **`ILLUSTRATIVE_ONLY` (Category D)**: The value or scenario is useful for explaining concepts but is NOT an established POLARIS engineering fact.
5. **`UNSUPPORTED` (Category E)**: The evidence inspected does not justify the claim.
6. **`MISREPRESENTED` (Category F)**: The source does not support the claim, or the claim materially misstates what the source establishes.

> [!CAUTION]
> **NO UPGRADING RULE**: Category D claims are NOT permitted to become Category C or A. Category C claims are NOT permitted to become Category A. Illustrative examples and synthetic assumptions MUST NOT become POLARIS engineering facts without empirical proof.
