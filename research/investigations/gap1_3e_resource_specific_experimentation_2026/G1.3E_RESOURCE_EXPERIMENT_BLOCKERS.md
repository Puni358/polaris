## 1. EXHAUSTIVE PER-CLASS BLOCKER AUDIT

### Class 1.1: Generator Operating State ($u(t) \in \{0, 1\}$)
- **Status**: `BLOCKED_DATA_UNAVAILABLE`
- **Blocker Description**: No high-speed digital SCADA event logs recording generator breaker trips (52a/52b auxiliary contact transitions) or engine shaft magnetic pickup RPM decay curves exist in the workspace repository.
- **Minimum Required Input**:
  1. Time-stamped SCADA telemetry logs ($\le 10\text{ ms}$ resolution) recording unexpected breaker trips under load.
  2. Measured network latency and jitter distribution for Nuolja station MODBUS/Ethernet gateways.
  3. Hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) breaker trip injection test harness.

### Class 1.2: Generator Active Power ($P_{\text{gen}}(t)$)
- **Status**: `BLOCKED_DATA_UNAVAILABLE`
- **Blocker Description**: No high-resolution active power time-series datasets capturing generator step-load acceptance, governor droop response, or thermal derating transients on Nuolja generator sets exist in the repository.
- **Minimum Required Input**:
  1. 1 Hz high-resolution 3-phase AC power meter recordings during severe load steps ($0 \to 100\text{ kW}$).
  2. Measured engine governor dynamic ramp rate bounds ($\frac{dP}{dt}$) under sub-zero polar operating conditions.

### Class 1.3: Generator Available Power / Capacity ($P_{\text{cap}}(t)$)
- **Status**: `BLOCKED_DATA_UNAVAILABLE`
- **Blocker Description**: No polar ambient temperature derating logs or air intake filter differential pressure restriction telemetry under polar blizzard conditions exist in the workspace.
- **Minimum Required Input**:
  1. Intake air manifold pressure and temperature differential sensor logs during polar blowing snow events.
  2. Thermal chamber test data for intake air filter icing/snow clogging dynamics on Caterpillar C9/C13 engines.

### Class 1.4: Fuel Tank Level ($L_{\text{fuel}}(t)$)
- **Status**: `BLOCKED_DATA_UNAVAILABLE`

  2. Dynamic calibration of tank fuel slosh noise and settling times under building wind loading.

### Class 1.5: Fuel Flow Rate ($\dot{m}_{\text{fuel}}(t)$)

## 2. BLOCKER RESOLUTION ROADMAP FOR FUTURE PHASES

To unblock physical experimentation in future research phases, the following field data acquisition campaign at Nuolja station is required:

1. **Deploy High-Speed SCADA Logger**: Install a 100 Hz data logger on generator breaker auxiliary contacts, AC power transducers, and CAN-bus gateways.
2. **Conduct Sub-Zero Thermal Decay Trial**: Disconnect block heater power during controlled maintenance window at $T_{\text{ambient}} < -30^\circ\text{C}$ and log $T_{\text{block}}$ decay over 12 hours.
3. **Calibrate Fuel Instruments**: Perform fuel flowmeter mass-balance verification and day-tank level slosh calibration with Arctic diesel fuel.
4. **HIL Bench Construction**: Build an isolated hardware-in-the-loop bench incorporating real engine ECU, switchgear aux contacts, and Modbus RTU/TCP gateways.
