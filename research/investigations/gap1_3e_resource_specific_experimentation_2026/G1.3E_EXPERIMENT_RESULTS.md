## 2. FINAL REPORT SYNTHESIS (SECTION 25 REQUIREMENTS)

### A. What was actually tested?
Experimental designs, ground-truth equations, delay operators, consequence metrics, and synthetic research formulations were systematically developed for all 8 canonical generator telemetry classes.

### B. What was only designed but not executed?
Physical hardware replay, historical dataset delay injection, hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) fault injections, and validated engine simulation executions were designed but **BLOCKED** from physical execution due to the total absence of measured physical time-series datasets and HIL rigs in the workspace repository.

### C. Which classes have real empirical evidence?
**Zero (0) classes** currently have real empirical field evidence in the repository. All 8 classes remain at Evidence Boundary C.

### D. Which classes remain blocked by missing data?
**All 8 classes** (1.1 through 1.8) remain blocked by missing physical SCADA logs, time-series measurements, and HIL test infrastructure.

### E. How does increasing telemetry age affect measured consequences in synthetic research models?
Synthetic research models demonstrate that increasing telemetry age introduces non-linear energy estimation error, conceals generator trips (Class 1.1), masks thermal derating (Class 1.2/1.3), obscures fuel line leaks (Class 1.4), masks engine block freezing (Class 1.6), and creates false-safe classifications during transient state transitions.

### F. Were effects steady-state or transition-specific?
Synthetic models reveal that degradation effects are heavily **transition-specific**. Telemetry age that introduces negligible error during steady-state operation causes catastrophic misclassification during rapid state transitions (e.g. breaker trip, fuel leak, block heater failure).

### G. Were there false-safe conditions?
Yes, synthetic models identify critical **false-safe conditions**:
- Stale `u=1` telemetry conceals a generator trip, causing POLARIS to falsely calculate $M_{\text{surv}} \ge 0$ when actual energy delivery is zero.
- Stale day-tank telemetry conceals a fuel leak, falsely reporting $6.0\text{ h}$ fuel sufficiency.
- Stale block temperature conceals heater loss, assuming an unstartable generator is dispatchable.

### H. What assumptions were synthetic?
All parameter values (100 kW genset capacity, 25 L/h fuel burn rate, 5%/min leak rate, 2.0°C/h thermal decay, 1.0 kW/10 min derate) are explicitly tagged `SYNTHETIC_ASSUMPTION` and are not Nuolja hardware facts.

### I. What evidence is genuinely Nuolja-specific?
**Zero (0) evidence** in this phase is Nuolja-specific. Nuolja transferability is marked `UNKNOWN` for Classes 1.1–1.7 and `YES` for Class 1.8 (universal LOTO governance principle).

### J. What remains required before a candidate freshness requirement could be proposed?
Real physical time-series data acquisition at Nuolja station, sub-zero block heater loss thermal trials, fuel slosh calibration, ECU J1939 fault injection logging, and a separate formal human governance review.
