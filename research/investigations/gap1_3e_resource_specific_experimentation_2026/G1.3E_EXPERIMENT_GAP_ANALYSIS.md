## 2. SYNTHETIC VS EMPIRICAL EVIDENCE BOUNDARY RULES

To maintain absolute scientific integrity, the gap analysis enforces strict firewall rules between synthetic research models and empirical evidence boundaries:

1. **Synthetic Models Cannot Advance Evidence Boundaries**: Synthetic stress models, sensitivity analyses, and simulated physical curves belong strictly to `EVIDENCE_LEVEL: MECHANISM_RESEARCH_ONLY`. They CANNOT move a class from Evidence Boundary C to Boundary B or A.
2. **Field Data Requirement for Boundary B**: Transitioning a class from Boundary C to Boundary B ("Candidate Research Evidence") requires verified physical time-series data from real hardware or validated HIL test rigs.
3. **Nuolja Hardware Requirement for Boundary A**: Transitioning a class from Boundary B to Boundary A ("Governance Ready") requires site-specific field validation on installed Nuolja station hardware under polar operating conditions.
