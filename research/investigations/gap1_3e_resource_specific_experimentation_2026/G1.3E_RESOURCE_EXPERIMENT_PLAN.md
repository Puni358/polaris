# POLARIS G1.3-E — RESOURCE-SPECIFIC EMPIRICAL EXPERIMENT PLAN

## Executive Summary & Governance Authorization Record

This document specifies the research design and empirical testing framework for **POLARIS G1.3-E Telemetry Freshness Experimentation**. 

In strict adherence to POLARIS human governance policy:
- **No universal freshness threshold** is assumed, selected, or tested as a default.
- **No production freshness rules** are created or promoted in this phase.
- All 8 canonical generator resource/measurement classes are evaluated independently.
- Ground-truth requirements, consequence metrics, and failure modes are explicitly specified per class.

## 1. CENTRAL RESEARCH QUESTION & METHODOLOGY

For each of the 8 canonical classes, the experimental framework addresses the central research question:

> **At what point, if any, does increasing telemetry age ($A = t_{\text{now}} - t_{\text{sample}}$) materially degrade the validity of the associated POLARIS state estimate, resource assessment, control decision, or 6-hour critical-load survivability assessment?**

### 1.1 Controlled Delay Injection Architecture

Experiments do not start with a predetermined threshold. Instead, telemetry age $A$ is varied across a wide controlled range to observe degradation regimes:

$$\text{Ground Truth } S(t) \longrightarrow \left[ \text{Delay Operator } \mathcal{D}(A) \right] \longrightarrow \text{Stale Observation } S(t - A) \longrightarrow \text{POLARIS EMS / Decision Engine}$$

Where the Delay Operator $\mathcal{D}(A)$ supports:
1. **Constant Delay**: Fixed delay $A = k$ seconds.
2. **Variable / Random Delay**: $A \sim \text{Uniform}(A_{\min}, A_{\max})$ or log-normal jitter.
3. **Burst Staleness**: Telemetry frozen at value $S(t_{\text{freeze}})$ for duration $T_{\text{burst}}$ during transient state changes.
4. **Communication Outage**: Complete loss of update packets ($A \to \infty$) with fallback to last known value.
5. **Stale-but-Plausible Injection**: Telemetry remains within physically plausible bounds but reflects a superseded operating state.

### 1.2 Ground-Truth vs Stale Observation
Every experiment requires an explicit reference:
- $\text{true\_state}(t)$: Authoritative, highest-frequency measured or simulated physical resource state.
- $\text{observed\_state}(t - A)$: Delayed telemetry received by the decision process.

If $\text{true\_state}(t)$ cannot be established due to missing physical logs or lack of physical sensor resolution, the experiment is marked:

$$\text{GROUND\_TRUTH\_INSUFFICIENT}$$

## 2. CANONICAL CLASS EXPERIMENTAL SPECIFICATIONS

The 8 canonical generator classes are defined below:

### Class 1.1: Generator Operating State ($u(t) \in \{0, 1\}$)
- **Physical Quantity**: Engine shaft rotation speed (RPM via magnetic pickup) and breaker contact position (52a/52b contacts).
- **Independent Variable**: Telemetry age $A \in [0\text{ s}, 3600\text{ s}]$ during breaker trips and uncommanded shutdowns.
- **Dependent Consequence Metric**: 
  - Overcredited Generator Energy: $E_{\text{overcredit}} = \int_{t_{\text{trip}}}^{t_{\text{trip}}+A} P_{\text{gen, cred}}(\tau) \, d\tau$ [kWh].
  - Critical-load energy deficit during 6h window ($M_{\text{surv}} < 0$ false-safe error).
- **Operational Scenarios**: 
  1. Mechanical engine trip under full load ($100\text{ kW}$).
  2. Overspeed electrical trip.
  3. Manual emergency stop button press.
  4. Steady-state online operation.
- **Ground Truth**: High-speed digital logger recording breaker aux contacts at $\le 1\text{ ms}$ resolution.

### Class 1.2: Generator Active Power ($P_{\text{gen}}(t)$)
- **Physical Quantity**: Active AC electric power output (kW) at generator terminals.
- **Independent Variable**: Telemetry age $A \in [0\text{ s}, 1800\text{ s}]$ during thermal derating and step-load changes.
- **Dependent Consequence Metric**: 
  - Active Power Estimation Error: $\epsilon_P(t) = |P_{\text{true}}(t) - P_{\text{observed}}(t - A)|$.
  - Integrated Energy Discrepancy over 6-hour horizon: $\Delta E_{\text{gen}} = \int_0^{6\text{h}} |\epsilon_P(\tau)| \, d\tau$ [kWh].
  - Unnecessary reserve dispatch or battery depletion.
- **Operational Scenarios**:
  1. Turbocharger efficiency loss causing $30\text{ kW}$ power drop over $60\text{ s}$.
  2. Rapid step-load acceptance ($0 \to 75\text{ kW}$ in $5\text{ s}$).
  3. Governor oscillation during severe wind/solar microgrid ramps.
- **Ground Truth**: Power analyzer recording 3-phase AC voltage and current at 100 Hz.

### Class 1.3: Generator Available Power / Capacity ($P_{\text{cap}}(t)$)
- **Physical Quantity**: Maximum continuous output capacity (kW) constrained by intake temperature and barometric pressure.
- **Independent Variable**: Telemetry age $A \in [0\text{ s}, 7200\text{ s}]$ during polar blizzard intake restriction.
- **Dependent Consequence Metric**:
  - Capacity Overestimation Error: $\Delta P_{\text{cap}} = \max(0, P_{\text{observed, cap}}(t - A) - P_{\text{true, cap}}(t))$.
  - Infeasible Dispatch Assignment: Dispatching $P_{\text{cmd}} > P_{\text{true, cap}}$ leading to frequency decay or engine stall.
- **Operational Scenarios**:
  1. Blizzard snow ingestion clogging air filter (intake vacuum increases, derating $100\text{ kW} \to 40\text{ kW}$ over 30 min).
  2. Sub-zero ambient temperature recovery ($T_{\text{ambient}}$ rise under polar sun).
- **Ground Truth**: Differential pressure transducer across intake filter + manifold temperature sensor.

### Class 1.4: Fuel Tank Level ($L_{\text{fuel}}(t)$)
- **Physical Quantity**: Day-tank fuel volume (Liters or % capacity).
- **Independent Variable**: Telemetry age $A \in [0\text{ s}, 21600\text{ s}]$ during tank leak or transfer pump failure.
- **Dependent Consequence Metric**:
  - Fuel Volume Estimation Error: $\epsilon_L(t) = L_{\text{true}}(t) - L_{\text{observed}}(t - A)$ [Liters].
  - Premature Generator Starvation: Unexpected generator shutdown prior to calculated $T_{\text{fuel\_depletion}}$.
  - False 6-hour survivability credit ($M_{\text{surv}} > 0$ when actual fuel $< 6\text{h}$ burn requirement).
- **Operational Scenarios**:
  1. Day-tank fuel line rupture ($5\text{\%/min}$ leak rate).
  2. Transfer pump failure with continuous full-load generator operation ($25\text{ L/h}$).
  3. Fuel slosh due to polar building vibration / wind loading.
- **Ground Truth**: Calibrated fuel mass scale / precision hydrostatic level transducer.

### Class 1.5: Fuel Flow Rate ($\dot{m}_{\text{fuel}}(t)$)
- **Physical Quantity**: Volumetric or mass fuel flow rate ($L/h$ or $kg/h$) in supply and return fuel lines.
- **Independent Variable**: Telemetry age $A \in [0\text{ s}, 3600\text{ s}]$ during cold fuel gelling or injector fouling.
- **Dependent Consequence Metric**:
  - BSFC Discrepancy: Deviation from calibrated fuel consumption curve.
  - Accelerated Fuel Depletion: Integrated fuel shortfall relative to baseline model.
- **Operational Scenarios**:
  1. Fuel gelling increasing return line backpressure and consumption by $+25\%$.
  2. Injector wear causing uneven fuel delivery.
- **Ground Truth**: Dual Coriolis mass flowmeters on supply and return lines logged at 1 Hz.

### Class 1.6: Startup Readiness Telemetry (OFF Generator)
- **Physical Quantity**: Starter battery DC voltage ($V_{\text{start}}$), engine block coolant temperature ($T_{\text{block}}$), fuel solenoid permissive bit.
- **Independent Variable**: Telemetry age $A \in [0\text{ s}, 28800\text{ s}]$ following block heater AC power loss.
- **Dependent Consequence Metric**:
  - Failed Start Misclassification: Treating unstartable generator ($T_{\text{block}} < -20^\circ\text{C}$) as dispatchable $u(t)=0 \to 1$.
  - Invalid Survivability Credit: Assigning $E_{\text{gen\_credit}} > 0$ to a frozen genset.
- **Operational Scenarios**:
  1. Block heater circuit breaker trip at $T_{\text{ambient}} = -40^\circ\text{C}$ ($T_{\text{block}}$ drops $2.0^\circ\text{C/h}$).
  2. Starter battery voltage decay under sub-zero self-discharge.
- **Ground Truth**: Multi-point block temperature thermocouples + battery impedance analyzer.

### Class 1.7: Generator Health / Fault Status
- **Physical Quantity**: ECU Diagnostic Trouble Codes (DTCs), low lube oil pressure switch, overspeed alarm.
- **Independent Variable**: Telemetry age $A \in [0\text{ s}, 600\text{ s}]$ during fault latching.
- **Dependent Consequence Metric**:
  - Missed Fault Duration: Window during which EMS dispatches or credits a faulted generator.
  - Generator Trip Upon Command: Attempting to start/load a generator with latched shutdown faults.
- **Operational Scenarios**:
  1. ECU latches low oil pressure fault code while generator is standby.
  2. High coolant temperature warning transitioning to shutdown latch.
- **Ground Truth**: Direct CAN-bus J1939 sniffer recording ECU diagnostic frames at 10 ms resolution.

### Class 1.8: Maintenance / Lockout State
- **Physical Quantity**: Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) key switch position, breaker manual trip handle, mechanical safety interlock contacts.
- **Independent Variable**: Hardwired contact state verification delay $A$.
- **Dependent Consequence Metric**:
  - Safety Governance Violation: Crediting or attempting to dispatch a generator under active LOTO.
  - Invalid Operator Permissive: Misrepresenting safety interlock state.
- **Operational Scenarios**:
  1. Maintenance technician turns LOTO switch to LOCKED OUT while SCADA reports AVAILABLE.
  2. Auxiliary contact bounce during manual maintenance switching.
- **Ground Truth**: Hardwired auxiliary contact loop verified via fail-closed current loop.

## 3. TAXONOMY OF TELEMETRY ANOMALIES

To prevent collapsing distinct failure modes into generic staleness, experiments must separate:

| Anomaly State | Definition | Formal EMS Handling |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Fresh & Valid** | $t_{\text{now}} - t_{\text{sample}} \le A_{\text{rule}}$, physical bounds pass. | Eligible for safety credit evaluation. |
| **Valid & Stale** | Structurally valid data packet, but $t_{\text{now}} - t_{\text{sample}} > A_{\text{rule}}$. | Zero safety credit ($0.0\text{ kWh}$); re-evaluate $M_{\text{surv}}$. |
| **Fresh & Invalid** | $t_{\text{now}} - t_{\text{sample}}$ recent, but out-of-bounds or physically impossible ($\frac{dP}{dt} > 50\text{ kW/s}$). | Zero safety credit ($0.0\text{ kWh}$); trigger sensor fault alarm. |
| **Missing** | No packet received within expected SCADA timeout window. | Zero safety credit ($0.0\text{ kWh}$); check remaining verified resources. |
| **Contradictory** | Telemetry sources disagree (e.g. breaker CLOSED but $P_{\text{gen}} = 0$). | Zero safety credit ($0.0\text{ kWh}$); flag hardware discrepancy. |
| **Unknown** | Freshness rule or verification status unestablished ($E_{\text{boundary}} = C$). | Zero safety credit ($0.0\text{ kWh}$); default conservative policy. |

## 4. NUOLJA TRANSFERABILITY REQUIREMENT

Every experiment must evaluate transferability to the Nuolja station environment:
- **Generic / OEM Spec**: Cannot be transferred to Nuolja without site SCADA latency, ambient wind/chill insulation data, and polar fuel blend testing.
- **Transferability Status Options**: `YES`, `NO`, or `UNKNOWN`.
- **Mandatory Justification**: Must cite specific Nuolja physical attributes or mark `UNKNOWN` if unmeasured.

---

## 5. THRESHOLD PROHIBITION & EVIDENCE PROMOTION

1. **No Numerical Freshness Selection**: Even if an experiment reveals degradation at $A = X\text{ s}$, no threshold equation $T_{\text{freshness}} = X$ shall be written into code or governance.
2. **Degradation Regime Documentation**: Results will be documented strictly as:
   $$\text{"Observed degradation regime begins around } X\text{ seconds under tested conditions."}$$
3. **Promotion Firewall**: Research evidence remains in `investigations/` and cannot alter production state without a separate, formal human governance review.
