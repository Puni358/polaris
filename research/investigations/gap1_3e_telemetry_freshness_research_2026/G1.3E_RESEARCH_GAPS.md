# POLARIS — G1.3-E RESEARCH GAPS & FUTURE WORK ROADMAP

## Executive Summary

This document details the specific research gaps, missing empirical data, and required testing protocols that remain prerequisites before G1.3-E freshness rules can be parameterized and authorized for production implementation, fulfilling **Research Requirements**.

---

## 1. IDENTIFIED RESEARCH GAPS

### Gap 1: Lack of Empirical Polar SCADA Jitter Logs
- **Description**: While satellite comms dropouts during polar blizzards are known conceptually, POLARIS lacks cataloged empirical packet loss time-series logs from active Antarctic stations (e.g., Mawson, Halley VI, McMurdo).
- **Impact**: Without comms jitter distributions, static timestamp rules risk triggering false generator credit invalidations during routine $30\text{-second}$ satellite buffer flushes.
- **Required Work**: Collect 12 months of high-resolution SCADA network ingress logs from Antarctic stations.

### Gap 2: Sensor Noise Floor & Zero-Variance Calibration
- **Description**: To implement stuck-value detection (detecting frozen SCADA payloads), POLARIS requires the physical noise floor ($\sigma_{\text{noise}}$) for hydrostatic fuel sensors and CT/PT power meters under polar station generator vibration.
- **Impact**: Setting a zero-variance window too tight causes false invalidations on highly stable digital meters; setting it too loose misses frozen SCADA buffers.
- **Required Work**: Run noise characterization tests on polar generator instrumentation testbeds.

### Gap 3: Cold-Start Engine Block Thermal Decay Data
- **Description**: For OFF generators ($u=0$), the rate of coolant thermal decay ($\frac{dT}{dt}$) following a block heater circuit breaker trip in sub-zero polar air ($-40^\circ\text{C}$ with wind chill) is unmodeled in empirical POLARIS logs.
- **Impact**: Start-readiness freshness requirements for OFF generators cannot be parameterized without thermal decay rates.
- **Required Work**: Conduct thermal decay logging on standby gensets in cold-chamber environmental test facilities.

### Gap 4: Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) Trip Latency Logging
- **Description**: Empirical latency distributions between ECU internal fault detection, relay tripping, Modbus register update, and SCADA master polling ingress are unmapped.
- **Impact**: Generator health telemetry freshness rules cannot be bounded to exact millisecond tolerances.
- **Required Work**: Perform HIL fault injection on Woodward and Caterpillar engine controllers.
