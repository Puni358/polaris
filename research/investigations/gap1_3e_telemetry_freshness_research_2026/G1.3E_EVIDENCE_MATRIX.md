# POLARIS — G1.3-E EVIDENCE SOURCE QUALITY & ASSURANCE MATRIX

## Executive Summary

This document evaluates available scientific, engineering, and operational evidence sources for establishing telemetry freshness specifications, fulfilling **Research Dimension 13**.

---

## 1. EVIDENCE QUALITY CLASSIFICATION TIER SYSTEM

POLARIS governance establishes four distinct evidence quality tiers:

*   **Class 1 Evidence (Authoritative Standard / Certified OEM Spec)**: Formally published standards (IEEE, IEC, NFPA, SAE) or manufacturer engine technical specifications. **Valid for establishing production safety rules.**
*   **Class 2 Evidence (Empirical Operational Log / HIL Test Data)**: Empirical telemetry logs from polar research stations or hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) testbed results. **Valid for parameterizing resource-specific freshness rules.**
*   **Class 3 Evidence (Theoretical Physics / Dynamic Model)**: Verified thermodynamic or electro-mechanical dynamic models ($\frac{dx}{dt}$ differential equations). **Valid for bounding uncertainty models.**
*   **Class 4 Evidence (Engineering Assumption / Rule of Thumb)**: Informal developer estimates, generic default values (e.g., $30\text{ s}$, $300\text{ s}$), or unverified heuristics. **STRICTLY PROHIBITED for production safety rules.**

## 3. EVIDENCE GAP ASSESSMENT FOR POLARIS

### 3.1 What Current Evidence Establishes:
1. **Standards (IEEE 1547 / IEC 61850)** establish that telemetry existence, structural validity, and quality flags are mandatory prerequisites before SCADA data can be ingested into automated control logic.
2. **OEM Engine Specifications** establish that fuel consumption rates and thermal derating follow non-linear physical functions that depend on load and ambient air temperature.
3. **Polar SCADA Logs** establish that satellite communication links experience periodic packet drops during severe weather, requiring telemetry freshness logic to distinguish comms latency from physical sensor failure.

### 3.2 What Current Evidence DOES NOT Establish:
1. **Manufacturer-Certified Freshness Limits**: No generator OEM publishes a single universal "maximum allowable telemetry age" for energy management algorithms.
2. **Polar-Station-Specific Sensor Drift Logs**: Empirical data on hydrostatic fuel sensor calibration drift under prolonged Antarctic station vibration is not yet cataloged in POLARIS archives.

---

## 4. CONCLUSION ON EVIDENCE ADEQUACY

> [!CAUTION]
> **GOVERNANCE CONCLUSION**: Available evidence is sufficient to prove that a universal freshness threshold is indefensible (Class 1 standards reject it), and sufficient to establish the 6-stage validation chain framework. However, **empirical Class 2 evidence is currently incomplete for setting exact numerical thresholds on specific polar generator hardware.**
>
> Therefore, POLARIS must maintain **G1.3-E in Boundary State C (Evidence Insufficient)** until Class 2 HIL or polar log data is gathered.
