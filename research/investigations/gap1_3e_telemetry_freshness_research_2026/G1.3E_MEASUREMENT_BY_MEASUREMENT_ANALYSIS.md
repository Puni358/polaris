# POLARIS — G1.3-E MEASUREMENT-BY-MEASUREMENT RESEARCH ANALYSIS

## Executive Summary

This document presents a granular, measurement-by-measurement physical and operational analysis for safety-critical generator telemetry in polar microgrids, fulfilling the scope of **Research Dimension 1 & Dimension 2**.

---

## 1. DETAILED MEASUREMENT ANALYSIS

### 1.1 Generator Operating State ($u(t) \in \{0, 1\}$)
- **Why Safety-Critical**: Determines whether the generator is currently online ($u=1$) or offline ($u=0$). Serves as the primary operational switch in $P_{\text{gen, credible}}(t)$.
- **Physical Quantity**: Engine shaft rotation speed (RPM) via magnetic pickup sensor and generator breaker contact state (52a/52b auxiliary switch status).
- **Potential Failure Mode**: Generator experiences a mechanical or electrical trip (e.g., over-current, reverse power, underspeed), opening the breaker. Stale telemetry reports $u=1$ when $u=0$.
- **Relevant Physical Dynamics ($\frac{dx}{dt}$)**: Instantaneous. Breaker opening occurs within $30\text{ ms} - 100\text{ ms}$.
- **Potential Freshness Criterion**: Continuous SCADA heartbeat + latching alarm state.
- **Evidence Supporting Criterion**: IEEE 1547.4 / IEC 61850 breaker status polling standards.
- **Evidence Limitations**: Polling rate constrained by serial link latency (e.g., Modbus RTU at 9600 baud).
- **Numerical Threshold Justified?**: **NO.** Threshold cannot be assigned without network latency measurements.
- **Required Future Validation**: Hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) trip injection testing.
- **Current POLARIS Status**: UNKNOWN / Uncredited if unestablished.

---

### 1.2 Generator Active Power ($P_{\text{gen}}(t)$)
- **Why Safety-Critical**: Primary real-time input to the $E_{\text{gen, credible}}$ integral.
- **Physical Quantity**: Active AC power output ($kW$) measured at generator terminals via current transformers (CTs) and potential transformers (PTs).
- **Potential Failure Mode**: Engine suffers thermal derating, fuel filter clogging, or turbocharger failure, causing active power output to drop below commanded dispatch.
- **Relevant Physical Dynamics ($\frac{dx}{dt}$)**: Fast to Moderate ($1.0\text{ kW/s} - 10.0\text{ kW/s}$).
- **Potential Freshness Criterion**: Moving variance check ($\sigma_P^2$) combined with timestamp age bound.
- **Evidence Supporting Criterion**: IEEE 1547 interconnection standards.
- **Evidence Limitations**: Does not model ambient temperature thermal decay.
- **Numerical Threshold Justified?**: **NO.**
- **Required Future Validation**: Step-load response testing on polar diesel gensets.
- **Current POLARIS Status**: UNKNOWN / Uncredited if unestablished.

---

### 1.3 Generator Available Power / Capacity ($P_{\text{cap}}(t)$)
- **Why Safety-Critical**: Caps maximum allowable $P_{\text{gen, credible}}(t)$ dispatch trajectory over the 6-hour horizon.
- **Physical Quantity**: Nameplate rating adjusted for ambient air temperature, combustion air intake temperature, and altitude derating curves.
- **Potential Failure Mode**: Blizzard conditions clog air intake filters or ambient temperature spikes, causing engine thermal overload if dispatched to nameplate capacity.
- **Relevant Physical Dynamics ($\frac{dx}{dt}$)**: Slow ($1.0\text{ kW / 10 min}$).
- **Potential Freshness Criterion**: Thermal model bounds + ambient temperature update age.
- **Evidence Supporting Criterion**: Caterpillar / Cummins polar engine derating specifications.
- **Evidence Limitations**: Depends on accuracy of external ambient temperature sensors.
- **Numerical Threshold Justified?**: **NO.**
- **Required Future Validation**: Thermal chamber testing under simulated polar airflow restrictions.
- **Current POLARIS Status**: UNKNOWN / Uncredited if unestablished.

---

### 1.4 Fuel Tank Level ($L_{\text{fuel}}(t)$)
- **Why Safety-Critical**: Primary input to $E_{\text{fuel, usable}}$ and fuel sufficiency constraint check ($E_{\text{fuel, deliverable}} \ge E_{\text{gen, credible}}$).
- **Physical Quantity**: Fuel volume ($Liters$ or $\%$ of day-tank capacity) measured via hydrostatic pressure transducers or ultrasonic level sensors.
- **Potential Failure Mode**: Fuel line rupture or day-tank leak occurs while stale telemetry reports a full tank, leading to unexpected generator starvation during an emergency.
- **Relevant Physical Dynamics ($\frac{dx}{dt}$)**: Very Slow under normal operation ($0.1\% / \text{minute}$); Fast under leak conditions ($5.0\% / \text{minute}$).
- **Potential Freshness Criterion**: Fuel volume balance integration ($\int P_{\text{gen}} \cdot \text{BSFC} \, dt$) matched against sensor reading.
- **Evidence Supporting Criterion**: Hydrostatic sensor manufacturer datasheets.
- **Evidence Limitations**: Liquid sloshing during station building vibration creates sensor noise.
- **Numerical Threshold Justified?**: **NO.**
- **Required Future Validation**: Polar fuel day-tank slosh and static settling calibration.
- **Current POLARIS Status**: UNKNOWN / Uncredited if unestablished.

---

### 1.5 Fuel Flow Rate ($\dot{m}_{\text{fuel}}(t)$)
- **Why Safety-Critical**: Validates Brake Specific Fuel Consumption (BSFC) curve and detects fuel system degradation.
- **Physical Quantity**: Volumetric or mass fuel flow ($L/h$ or $kg/h$) in supply and return fuel lines.
- **Potential Failure Mode**: Fuel injector fouling or cold fuel gelling increases BSFC, exhausting fuel faster than modeled.
- **Relevant Physical Dynamics ($\frac{dx}{dt}$)**: Moderate ($10\text{ s} - 60\text{ s}$).
- **Potential Freshness Criterion**: Mass-balance verification between supply and return meters.
- **Evidence Supporting Criterion**: Coriolis flowmeter specifications.
- **Evidence Limitations**: High-latitude fuel viscosity variations alter flowmeter calibration.
- **Numerical Threshold Justified?**: **NO.**
- **Required Future Validation**: Low-temperature diesel fuel viscosity flow test.
- **Current POLARIS Status**: UNKNOWN / Uncredited if unestablished.

---

### 1.6 Startup Readiness Telemetry (OFF Generator)
- **Why Safety-Critical**: Prerequisite under **G1.2 & G3** for crediting an OFF generator ($u(t_0)=0$) with 6-hour survivability energy.
- **Physical Quantity**: Starter battery DC voltage ($V_{\text{start}}$), engine block coolant temperature ($T_{\text{block}}$), fuel solenoid valve permissive bit, and auto-start mode selector switch state.
- **Potential Failure Mode**: Block heater fails in sub-zero polar temperatures ($T_{\text{block}} < 20^\circ\text{C}$), causing engine cranking failure or lube oil starvation upon start command.
- **Relevant Physical Dynamics ($\frac{dx}{dt}$)**: Slow thermal decay ($2.0^\circ\text{C / hour}$).
- **Potential Freshness Criterion**: Multi-permissive boolean verification gate.
- **Evidence Supporting Criterion**: NFPA 110 (Standard for Emergency and Standby Power Systems) & OEM Cold-Start Specs.
- **Evidence Limitations**: Temperature sensor placement may not reflect cylinder wall freezing.
- **Numerical Threshold Justified?**: **NO.**
- **Required Future Validation**: Sub-zero block heater failure thermal decay test.
- **Current POLARIS Status**: UNKNOWN / Uncredited if unestablished.

---

### 1.7 Generator Health / Fault Status
- **Why Safety-Critical**: Prevents dispatching or crediting a generator with active internal faults.
- **Physical Quantity**: Engine Control Unit (ECU) Diagnostic Trouble Codes (DTCs), lube oil pressure, coolant temperature, overspeed status.
- **Potential Failure Mode**: Low oil pressure fault latches in ECU, but stale telemetry reports "No Active Alarms," resulting in optimistic credit for a generator that will trip immediately upon load acceptance.
- **Relevant Physical Dynamics ($\frac{dx}{dt}$)**: Instantaneous fault latching ($< 1.0\text{ s}$).
- **Potential Freshness Criterion**: Active latching diagnostic check + heartbeat.
- **Evidence Supporting Criterion**: SAE J1939 ECU CAN-bus protocol standards.
- **Evidence Limitations**: Internal ECU buffer delay before broadcast.
- **Numerical Threshold Justified?**: **NO.**
- **Required Future Validation**: ECU CAN-bus fault injection logging.
- **Current POLARIS Status**: UNKNOWN / Uncredited if unestablished.

---

### 1.8 Maintenance / Lockout State
- **Why Safety-Critical**: Ensures human safety and prevents crediting out-of-service generators.
- **Physical Quantity**: Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) key switch position, local breaker manual trip switch state.
- **Potential Failure Mode**: Maintenance technician opens breaker for service; stale SCADA reports generator as available.
- **Relevant Physical Dynamics ($\frac{dx}{dt}$)**: Manual / Instantaneous.
- **Potential Freshness Criterion**: Hardwired auxiliary contact interlock bit.
- **Evidence Supporting Criterion**: OSHA LOTO 1910.147 & IEEE 1547.
- **Evidence Limitations**: Mechanical switch contact bounce or wear.
- **Numerical Threshold Justified?**: **NO.**
- **Required Future Validation**: Auxiliary switch contact integrity check.
- **Current POLARIS Status**: UNKNOWN / Uncredited if unestablished.
