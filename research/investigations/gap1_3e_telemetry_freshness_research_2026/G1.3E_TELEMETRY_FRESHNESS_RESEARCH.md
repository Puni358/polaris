## 1. CORE RESEARCH FINDINGS

### 1.1 The Fundamental Technical Invalidation of Universal Telemetry Age
A universal numerical telemetry freshness threshold (such as $30\text{ s}$, $300\text{ s}$, or $900\text{ s}$) is **scientifically indefensible** for polar microgrid energy management.

Different physical safety-critical measurements operate on physical dynamics ($\frac{dx}{dt}$) that span **four orders of magnitude**:
- **Generator Operating/Fault Relay State**: Instantaneous ($\Delta t < 100\text{ ms}$). Stale health telemetry for even $10\text{ seconds}$ can mask a breaker trip during a emergency battery condition.
- **Active Generation Output ($P_{\text{gen}}$)**: Dynamic ($\Delta t \approx 1\text{ s} - 10\text{ s}$). Fluctuates with engine governor control and thermal derating.
- **Day-Tank Fuel Level ($L_{\text{fuel}}$)**: Slow ($\Delta t \approx 10\text{ min} - 60\text{ min}$). Fuel depletion at $25\text{ L/h}$ in a $500\text{ L}$ day-tank changes state at $< 0.1\%\text{ per minute}$ under constant load.

Applying a single $30\text{-second}$ threshold across all telemetry causes unnecessary telemetry invalidation for slow variables (such as fuel level during routine satellite communication drops), while applying a $5\text{-minute}$ threshold allows stale generator health telemetry to mask catastrophic trips.

## 5. ANALYSIS OF THE CRITICAL QUESTION

### Static Timestamp Age ($\Delta t \le X$) vs. State Uncertainty Tolerance ($\sigma_{\text{state}}(t) \le \epsilon_{\text{surv}}$)

The research conclusively proves that defining freshness purely as a timestamp constraint ($\Delta t \le X$) is **scientifically inferior** to an **Uncertainty-Bounded Freshness Criterion**.

#### Mathematical Formulation of Uncertainty-Bounded Freshness:
Telemetry remains safety-eligible for survivability calculations if and only if the cumulative uncertainty in the physical state variable $x(t)$, propagated over the 6-hour horizon $T_{\text{surv}}$, satisfies:

$$\sigma_{E_{\text{gen}}}(t_{\text{now}}, \Delta t_{\text{age}}) = \int_{0}^{6\text{h}} \sigma_{P_{\text{gen}}}(t; \Delta t_{\text{age}}) \, dt \le \epsilon_{\text{surv\_tolerance}}$$

Where $\epsilon_{\text{surv\_tolerance}}$ is the maximum allowable energy error that maintains $M_{\text{surv}} \ge 0$ with $99\%$ confidence.

#### Required Evidence to Operationalize Uncertainty-Bounded Freshness:
1. Physical drift model $\frac{d\sigma^2}{dt}$ for each resource state under open-loop conditions.
2. Verified error bounds for sensor measurement noise $\sigma_{\text{meas}}$.
3. Upper-bound rate of fuel consumption variance under polar ambient swings.
