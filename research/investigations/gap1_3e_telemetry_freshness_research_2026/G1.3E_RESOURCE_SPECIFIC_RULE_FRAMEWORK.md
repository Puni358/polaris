## 3. MULTI-GENERATOR EVALUATION PROTOCOL

When a polar microgrid deploys multiple generators with heterogeneous SCADA capabilities (e.g., Gen 1 with modern CAN-bus ECU, Gen 2 with legacy analog meters), the `FreshnessEligible()` interface evaluates each resource independently:

$$\text{E}_{\text{gen, credible, total}} = \sum_{i=1}^{N_{\text{gens}}} \text{E}_{\text{gen, credible}, i} \cdot \mathbb{I}\left[\text{FreshnessEligible}(\text{gen}_i, \dots) = \text{True}\right]$$

*   If Gen 1 telemetry is fresh and valid $\implies E_{\text{gen, credible}, 1} > 0$.
*   If Gen 2 telemetry freshness cannot be established $\implies E_{\text{gen, credible}, 2} = 0.0\text{ kWh}$.
*   Total credible generator energy equals the sum of valid generator credits, capped at the critical load requirement $E_{\text{critical}} = 69.60\text{ kWh}$.
