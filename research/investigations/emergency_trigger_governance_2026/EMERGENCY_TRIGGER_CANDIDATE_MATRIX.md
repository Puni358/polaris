# POLARIS — EMERGENCY TRIGGER CANDIDATE EVALUATION MATRIX

| Candidate ID | Trigger Candidate Family | Conceptual Formula / Logic | Classification | Tradeoffs & Considerations | Safety & Operational Assessment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A.1** | **Immediate Critical Load Shortfall** | $P_{\text{critical, served}} < P_{\text{critical, required}}$ for 1 interval (e.g. 15 min) | **HARD IMMEDIATE TRIGGER** | Ensures zero delay in recognizing an actual blackout/under-frequency condition on critical buses. High risk of false triggers from transient telemetry glitches or single-step sensor dropouts. | **Mandatory fallback/safety trigger.** Must be backed up by physical relay action regardless of software EMS state. |
| **A.2** | **Persisted Critical Load Shortfall** | $P_{\text{critical, served}} < P_{\text{critical, required}}$ for $N$ consecutive intervals (e.g. $N \ge 2$) | **PERSISTENCE-BASED TRIGGER** | Filters out transient telemetry noise and sub-interval sensor dropouts. Slight delay (e.g. 15–30 min) before formal state transition, though hardware protection reacts instantly. | **Recommended for software state machine.** Prevents mode chatter while acknowledging true unserved load. |
| **B.1** | **Imminent Survivability Exhaustion** | $E_{\text{survivable}} < E_{\text{critical, 6h}}$ where $E_{\text{survivable}} = E_{\text{batt, usable}} + E_{\text{gen, avail}}$ | **PERSISTENCE-BASED TRIGGER** | Proactively enters Emergency Mode *before* actual load shedding occurs, allowing Option C load curtailment to extend battery life. Requires reliable estimation of net available energy. | **High Value.** Protects 6-hour critical survivability requirement when available generation cannot maintain battery reserve. |
| **C.1** | **Static Low SOC Floor (e.g. SOC < 20%)** | $SOC \le SOC_{\text{physical, min}}$ (20% SOC / 40.0 kWh) | **HARD IMMEDIATE TRIGGER** | Simple, robust, immune to complex model errors. Directly guards physical battery depletion limit. | **Essential lower safety net.** Triggers emergency load shedding before battery deep-discharge lockout. |
| **C.2** | **SOC Rate of Decline Trajectory** | $\frac{dSOC}{dt} < -\text{threshold}$ AND $t_{\text{to\_min}} < 6\text{ h}$ | **CONTRIBUTING CONDITION** | Detects rapid battery collapse under high demand and low generation. High sensitivity to short-term load spikes if used in isolation. | **Best used as a contributing condition** to trigger survivability re-evaluation rather than a standalone hard trigger. |
| **D.1** | **Complete Diesel Generator Loss** | $P_{\text{diesel, max}} = 0$ (Failure / Fuel Exhaustion / Lockout) | **PERSISTENCE-BASED TRIGGER** | Polar microgrid relies on diesel as the primary dispatchable backup. Total loss of diesel while battery is in deficit severely threatens survivability. | **High criticality.** Should trigger Emergency if battery SOC is below normal target or renewable generation is insufficient. |
| **D.2** | **Renewable Generation Drop/Loss** | $P_{\text{solar}} + P_{\text{wind}} = 0$ | **NOT SUITABLE AS A TRIGGER** | Solar/wind loss is normal (nighttime, low wind). Diesel and battery are designed to handle renewable intermittency. | **Normal operating condition.** Must NOT trigger Emergency. Belongs in standard economic dispatch. |
| **D.3** | **Multiple Resource Failure** | Diesel Unavailable AND Battery SOC < 37.83% | **HARD IMMEDIATE TRIGGER** | Simultaneous loss of dispatchable generation and depleted battery reserve creates an existential threat to critical load. | **Critical compound trigger.** Requires immediate Option C load curtailment to preserve remaining battery capacity. |
| **E.1** | **Persistent Unrecovered Reserve Deficit** | $SOC < 37.83\%$ for $> T_{\text{max\_deficit}}$ (e.g., 24 hours) | **WARNING / PRE-EMERGENCY** | Detects long-term inability to replenish battery reserve due to prolonged resource drought or undersized generation. | Creates an operator warning/alert. Should transition to Emergency only if net energy balance is negative. |
| **F.1** | **Forecast-Based Imminent Deficit** | $\hat{E}_{\text{survivable}}(t + h) < E_{\text{critical, 6h}}$ over forecast horizon $h$ | **WARNING / PRE-EMERGENCY** | Enables early advisory and pre-emptive reserve restoration dispatch. Vulnerable to forecast error/uncertainty. | **Advisory/Optimization Input.** Excellent for triggering pre-emergency alerts, but should not trigger hard load shedding without physical validation. |
