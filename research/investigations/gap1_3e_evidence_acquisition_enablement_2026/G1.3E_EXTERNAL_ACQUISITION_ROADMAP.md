# POLARIS G1.3-E — EXTERNAL ACQUISITION ROADMAP

## Executive Summary & Governance Record

This document outlines the prioritized roadmap for acquiring external evidence sources to unblock empirical experiments across the 8 canonical generator classes.

In strict compliance with POLARIS governance:
- **Prioritization Methodology**: Candidate acquisition routes are ranked by accessibility, verified content, evidence quality, applicability, and expected experimental value.
- **Firewall Freshness Rules**: This roadmap defines an **external evidence acquisition path**, NOT a freshness threshold policy.
- **Availability Rigor**: Differentiates between external availability (`PUBLICLY_ACCESSIBLE`, `REQUESTABLE`) and local workspace presence (`workspace_copy: false`).
