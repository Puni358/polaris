## 2. STRICT SYNTHETIC DATA & THRESHOLD FIREWALLS

In strict compliance with POLARIS governance:
1. **Synthetic Data Firewall**: Synthetic stress models and MILP dispatch outputs belong strictly to `EVIDENCE_LEVEL: MECHANISM_RESEARCH_ONLY`. They CANNOT be used to claim empirical freshness requirements, Nuolja behavior, field performance, or validated safety limits.
2. **Threshold Promotion Prohibition**: No activity in this phase creates, implies, or promotes a POLARIS telemetry freshness threshold.
3. **Epistemic Labeling**: Sampling rates, timestamp precision targets, and instrumentation frequencies define candidate research parameters; they must **NEVER** be converted into maximum tolerable telemetry age rules ($T_{\text{freshness}}$).
