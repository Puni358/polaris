# POLARIS G1.3-E — EVIDENCE ACQUISITION ROADMAP

## Executive Summary & Governance Record

This document outlines the prioritized evidence acquisition roadmap for unblocking resource-specific empirical experiments across the 8 canonical generator classes.

In strict compliance with POLARIS governance:
- **Class-by-Class Progress**: It is not required to solve all 8 classes simultaneously. Progress can be achieved incrementally as datasets are acquired.
- **Candidate Research Instrumentation**: Quantitative instrumentation specifications (e.g. proposed $1\text{ Hz}$ level logging, $10\text{ ms}$ switchgear logging) are explicitly designated as **Candidate Experimental Design Parameters**. They do NOT establish scientific minimums, safety requirements, or telemetry freshness thresholds.
- **Unverified Primary Sources**: External references (Caterpillar specs, SAE J1939 protocols, OSHA LOTO regulations) represent candidate research models (`EXPERIMENT_DESIGN_PARAMETER`) because primary underlying source files are absent from the workspace repository.
- **No Threshold Promotion**: This roadmap defines an **evidence acquisition path**, NOT a freshness threshold policy.

## 1. TECHNICAL PRIORITIZATION MATRIX

Acquisition priorities are assigned based on explicit technical criteria:
1. **Safety Relevance**: Direct impact on safety credit and 6-hour critical-load survivability.
2. **Relevance to Survivability**: Magnitude of potential energy overcrediting error ($E_{\text{overcredit}}$).
3. **Acquisition Feasibility**: Cost, complexity, and availability of required instrumentation.
4. **Ground-Truth Decoupling**: Feasibility of establishing an independent, authoritative reference state.
5. **Reproducibility**: Ability of independent researchers to replicate datasets and delay replay experiments.

| Priority | Class ID | Canonical Measurement Class | Unblocking Tier | Primary Blocker | Proposed Acquisition Route (Candidate Research Design Parameters) | Expected Outcome |
|:---:|:---:|:---|:---:|:---|:---|:---|
| **P1** | **1.4** | Fuel Tank Level ($L_{\text{fuel}}$) | **Tier 1** | Missing day-tank slosh & leak logs. | Candidate Route: Hydrostatic level logger (proposed $\sim 1\text{ Hz}$ logging) on day-tank with slosh filter. | Unblocks Class 1.4 experiment (`TIER_0_EXECUTABLE`). |
| **P2** | **1.6** | Startup Readiness (OFF Gen) | **Tier 2** | Missing sub-zero block heater loss thermal logs. | Candidate Route: Controlled block heater power interruption test at sub-zero ambient ($T_{\text{ambient}} < -30^\circ\text{C}$ synthetic scenario). | Validates block thermal decay model & unblocks Class 1.6. |

## 2. MINIMUM VIABLE EVIDENCE PATH (INCREMENTAL UNBLOCKING)

The minimum viable evidence path demonstrates how POLARIS can achieve experimental executability class-by-class without requiring a single massive campaign:

```
[ Phase 1: High-Feasibility Field Logging ]
  → Acquire Candidate Day-Tank Level (~1 Hz) & Fuel Flow Logs (~1 Hz) (P1, P4)
  → Conduct Candidate Sub-Zero Block Heater Power Loss Trial (P2)
  ↓
  Result: Unblocks Classes 1.4, 1.6, and 1.5 to TIER_0_EXECUTABLE

[ Phase 2: Switchgear & Power Instrumentation ]
  → Connect Candidate Power Analyzer (~1 Hz) & Digital Switchgear Logger (~10 ms) (P3, P5)
  ↓
  Result: Unblocks Classes 1.2 and 1.8 to TIER_0_EXECUTABLE

[ Phase 3: ECU & HIL Bench Construction ]
  → Build Isolated HIL Bench with Candidate CAN Logger (~10 ms) & Breaker Trip Injector (P6, P7)
  → Install Intake Filter Vacuum Transducer for Polar Blizzard Tracking (P8)
  ↓
  Result: Unblocks Classes 1.1, 1.7, and 1.3 to TIER_0_EXECUTABLE
```
