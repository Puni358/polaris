## 1. REPOSITORY EVIDENCE ASSET AUDIT

Each candidate asset in the workspace has been evaluated against the 7 mandatory usability criteria defined in Section 7 of POLARIS G1.3-E enablement governance:

1. **Temporal Information**: Can telemetry age ($A = t_{\text{now}} - t_{\text{sample}}$) be reconstructed?
2. **Ground Truth**: Is an authoritative reference state available?
3. **Resource Semantics**: Does the asset represent the physical generator measurement under investigation?
4. **Temporal Resolution**: Is the sample rate adequate for delay injection experiments?
5. **Provenance**: Are metadata, units, and collection methods documented?
6. **Scenario Coverage**: Does the asset capture operating transitions (trips, load steps, leaks, block heater loss)?
7. **Reproducibility**: Can another researcher independently obtain and replay the dataset?
