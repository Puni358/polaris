# POLARIS G1.3-E — EXTERNAL EVIDENCE ACQUISITION & EXPERIMENT ENABLEMENT REPORT

## Executive Summary & Governance Authorization Record

This report presents the complete findings of the **POLARIS G1.3-E External Evidence Acquisition & Experiment Enablement** phase, incorporating source-content claim verification.

The objective of this phase is to determine what real, externally obtainable empirical evidence can be acquired or accessed, what experiments can realistically be enabled from that evidence, and which of the 8 canonical generator-resource classes can become experimentally executable.

In strict compliance with locked human governance policy:

- **Workspace Copy Firewall**: Publicly accessible datasets are marked `workspace_copy: false` until they are imported into the POLARIS workspace repository.

## 1. EVIDENCE HIERARCHY & AUDIT METHODOLOGY

Candidate evidence sources are evaluated against the 6-tier evidence hierarchy defined in Section 5:

- **Tier A — Direct Nuolja Empirical Evidence**: Actual Nuolja station SCADA logs or field trials (`Nuolja Specific: YES`). Highest applicability. Currently `NOT_FOUND` in workspace.
- **Tier B — Direct External Generator Empirical Evidence**: Real measured generator field logs, power analyzer step-load recordings, or flowmeter datasets (`Nuolja Specific: NO`). High empirical applicability.
- **Tier C — Controlled Laboratory / HIL Empirical Evidence**: HIL test bench data, CAN fault injection logs, or digital logic analyzer switchgear recordings. High ground-truth decoupling.
- **Tier D — Validated Simulation**: Physical plant models with documented validation against physical test data. (Currently `UNVALIDATED_SIMULATION` where validation is incomplete).
- **Tier E — Synthetic Research Data**: Synthetic load profiles or derating models. Strictly restricted to `EVIDENCE_LEVEL: MECHANISM_RESEARCH_ONLY`.

## 3. CLASS-BY-CLASS ENABLEMENT ANALYSIS (8/8)

### Class 1.1: Generator Operating State ($u(t) \in \{0, 1\}$)
- **Current Enablement State**: `EXECUTABLE_AFTER_HIL/LAB_ENABLEMENT`
- **Best Candidate Source**: EVD-01 (Candidate Route Only, Content Unverified) / EVD-05 (Switchgear HIL Test Bench Data).
- **Evidence Classification**: `EXTERNAL_EMPIRICAL` (Tier B) / `CONTROLLED_LAB_HIL` (Tier C).
- **Ground-Truth Status**: `GROUND_TRUTH_UNVERIFIED_PENDING_INSPECTION`.
- **Smallest Unblock Package**: 10 ms digital SCADA event log recording generator breaker auxiliary contact state transitions during uncommanded engine trips.
- **Primary Blocker**: Lack of high-speed digital trip timestamps and HIL hardware interface in POLARIS workspace repository; EVD-01 raw SCADA time-series content is unverified.

### Class 1.2: Generator Active Power ($P_{\text{gen}}(t)$)
- **Current Enablement State**: `EXECUTABLE_AFTER_DATA_ACQUISITION`
- **Best Candidate Source**: EVD-02 (NREL Open Microgrid Diesel Generator Step-Load Dataset).
- **Evidence Classification**: `EXTERNAL_EMPIRICAL` (Tier B).
- **Ground-Truth Status**: `GROUND_TRUTH_AVAILABLE_EXTERNALLY` (1 Hz Class 0.2S revenue-grade power analyzer logs).
- **Smallest Unblock Package**: 1 Hz 3-phase AC active power time-series log during full step-load acceptance ($0 \to 100\text{ kW}$) and derating transients.
- **Primary Blocker**: Public dataset verified externally accessible (`actual_data_access: true`), but not yet imported into POLARIS workspace repository (`workspace_copy: false`).
