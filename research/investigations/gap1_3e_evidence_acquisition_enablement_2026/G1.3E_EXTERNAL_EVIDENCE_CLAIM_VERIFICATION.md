# POLARIS G1.3-E — EXTERNAL EVIDENCE CLAIM VERIFICATION REPORT

## Executive Summary & Governance Record

This report presents the complete findings of the **POLARIS G1.3-E External Evidence Claim Verification** phase.

The objective of this phase is to execute a rigorous, source-content verification of the six candidate external evidence sources (EVD-01 through EVD-06) identified during the enablement phase to determine:

> **What do these external sources actually contain, and what experiment-enablement claims are genuinely supported by the evidence?**

In strict compliance with locked POLARIS governance:

- **Zero Workspace Downloads**: No datasets were downloaded or imported into the POLARIS workspace during this verification step (`workspace_copy: false`).

## 1. VERIFICATION METHODOLOGY

Each candidate external evidence source was independently audited across the complete verification chain:

$$\text{Source Existence} \longrightarrow \text{Actual Accessibility} \longrightarrow \text{Content Inspection} \longrightarrow \text{Variable Verification} \longrightarrow \text{Ground Truth} \longrightarrow \text{Generator Applicability}$$

Verification State Definitions:
1. `DIRECTLY_VERIFIED`: Actual resource directly inspected and relevant content confirmed. (0 sources)
2. `PARTIALLY_VERIFIED`: Resource exists and some relevant properties confirmed, but important claims unverified/unsupported. (2 sources: EVD-02, EVD-04)
3. `ACCESSIBLE_BUT_CONTENT_UNVERIFIED`: Access path verified, but raw time-series files require formal request or NDA. (3 sources: EVD-01, EVD-03, EVD-05)
4. `SOURCE_NOT_VERIFIED`: Candidate resource could not be independently located or confirmed. (1 source: EVD-06)
