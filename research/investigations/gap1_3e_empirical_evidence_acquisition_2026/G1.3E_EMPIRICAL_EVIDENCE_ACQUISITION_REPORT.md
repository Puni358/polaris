## 1. CANONICAL RESOURCE & MEASUREMENT CLASS AUDIT

The 8 canonical generator resource/measurement classes currently governed under G1.3-E have been systematically audited for empirical evidence:

### 1.1 Generator Operating State ($u(t) \in \{0, 1\}$)
- **Physical Quantity**: Engine shaft rotation speed (RPM) via magnetic pickup and generator breaker contact state (52a/52b aux contacts).
- **Failure / Decision Context**: Generator mechanical/electrical trip occurs while stale telemetry reports online state ($u=1$). POLARIS overestimates $P_{\text{gen, credible}}(t)$ and credits unsupplied energy during 6-hour survivability evaluation.
- **Evidence Found**: YES (Reference level: IEEE 1547.4 / IEC 61850).
- **Source ID**: SRC-01 / SRC-02 (`REFERENCE_VERIFIED_BUT_NUMERICAL_SUPPORT_UNVERIFIED`).
- **Numerical Timing Claim**: 30–100 ms breaker trip speed; 1–5 s SCADA polling rate.
- **Timing Meaning**: Physical disconnect speed & network transmission frequency; NOT a safety-credit freshness validity limit.
- **Validated Freshness Requirement**: NO (`NO_VALIDATED_FRESHNESS_REQUIREMENT_FOUND`).
- **Nuolja Transferability**: `UNKNOWN` (Requires Nuolja SCADA latency measurement).
- **Production Eligible**: NO.

### 1.2 Generator Active Power ($P_{\text{gen}}(t)$)
- **Physical Quantity**: Active AC power output (kW) measured at generator terminals via CTs/PTs.
- **Failure / Decision Context**: Engine thermal derating, turbocharger failure, or fuel restriction causes active power to drop below dispatch command while stale telemetry reports full output.
- **Evidence Found**: YES (Reference level: IEEE 1547 / IEC 61850).
- **Source ID**: SRC-01 / SRC-02 (`REFERENCE_VERIFIED_BUT_NUMERICAL_SUPPORT_UNVERIFIED`).
- **Numerical Timing Claim**: 1.0–10.0 kW/s physical ramp / derate rate.
- **Timing Meaning**: Physical dynamic power rate ($\frac{dP}{dt}$); NOT a telemetry age validity threshold.
- **Validated Freshness Requirement**: NO (`NO_VALIDATED_FRESHNESS_REQUIREMENT_FOUND`).
- **Nuolja Transferability**: `UNKNOWN` (Requires polar step-load trial).
- **Production Eligible**: NO.

### 1.3 Generator Available Power / Capacity ($P_{\text{cap}}(t)$)

## 2. CRITICAL DISTINCTION: SAMPLE RATE $\neq$ FRESHNESS REQUIREMENT

A central finding of this evidence acquisition phase is the absolute necessity of distinguishing between instrument operational parameters and safety validity bounds:

$$\text{Sample Rate} \neq \text{Update Interval} \neq \text{Fault Latency} \neq \text{Freshness Requirement}$$

1. **Sensor Sampling Interval**: Frequency at which an analog transducer digitizes a physical signal (e.g. 10 s for hydrostatic level sensor).
2. **Communication Update Interval**: Frequency at which a SCADA gateway broadcasts data packets (e.g. 1–5 s for Modbus, 100 ms for J1939 CAN).
3. **Controller Execution Period**: Main loop execution cycle of the microgrid controller (e.g. 100 ms).
4. **Fault Detection Latency**: Duration required for ECU algorithms to latch a diagnostic fault code (e.g. < 1.0 s).
5. **Protection Response Time**: Mechanical disconnect speed of protective breakers (e.g. 30–100 ms).
6. **Telemetry Age**: Wall-clock time elapsed since timestamp generation ($t_{\text{now}} - t_{\text{sample}}$).
7. **Maximum Tolerable Telemetry Age ($T_{\text{freshness}}$)**: Scientifically validated duration beyond which telemetry staleness introduces unacceptable risk to critical-load survivability.
8. **Required Decision Horizon**: The 6.0-hour critical-load evaluation window ($T_{\text{surv}}$).

None of the cited standards (IEEE 1547, IEC 61850, SAE J1939, NFPA 110) or OEM datasheets specify item #7 ($T_{\text{freshness}}$). Recording items #1–5 as item #7 is scientifically invalid and prohibited by POLARIS governance.
